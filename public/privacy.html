Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Athera Talks is a chat app made by Taha Ahsan. This page explains what the app stores and how that information is used. You can read it without signing in.

What the app stores

  • Your email address, username, and password if you create an email account.
  • Google account details, if you choose Continue with Google.
  • A profile photo, favourite anime, theme, and wallpaper, if you set them.
  • Messages, voice messages, and photos you send, plus who you chat with.
  • The connection details needed to place a voice call. The microphone is used for voice messages and calls.

How it is used

This information is used to run chat, voice messages, and voice calls, and to keep your account secure. It is not sold and it is not used for advertising.

Supabase stores accounts, messages, and files. Google handles Continue with Google when that option is on. Metered can relay call audio when the two phones cannot connect directly.

How long it is kept

Information stays while the account is active. To delete an account, email ahsantaha16@gmail.com.
