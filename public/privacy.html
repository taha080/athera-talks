Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026
Last updated: October 19, 2026

Athera Talks is a chat app made by Taha Ahsan. This page explains what the app collects, how that information is used, and who handles it. You can read it without signing in.

What the app collects

Account information:

  • Your email address.
  • Your username. This is the name shown on your profile and in chats.
  • A profile photo, if you upload one.
  • If you choose Continue with Google, the sign-in details Google sends. Those details include your email and the name on your Google account. They are used to create the account and sign you in.
  • A password, if you create an account with email. It is stored in a protected form so you can sign in.

You can also save a favourite anime, a colour theme, a wallpaper (including a photo you upload), and your choices for last seen, read receipts, and notifications. The app stores the time you were last here, and whether a message was delivered or read, including when you hide those from other people.

Who can see last seen and read receipts

Last seen and read receipts can be turned off in Privacy settings. Other people in your chats then cannot see when you were last here, or the delivered and read ticks on their messages to you. Note: the app creator (Taha Ahsan) can always see last seen and read receipts.

Messages, contacts, and calls:

  • Text messages you send.
  • Voice messages and photos you send, including the length of a voice message.
  • Replies, reactions, and whether a message was delivered or read.
  • Usernames you search, contact requests, and your list of chats.
  • Basic technical details needed to place a voice call, including who is calling and the connection data the two devices exchange. The microphone is used only during the call. A live call is carried for the length of that call. A voice message is saved so the other person can play it later.

How the data is used

This information is used only to run chat, voice messages, and voice calls, and to keep accounts secure. That includes signing you in, resetting a password, and delivering messages and calls to the people you choose. It is not used for advertising.

Sharing

Your information is not sold. It is not shared with third parties for advertising. It is stored and processed by the services that run the app:

  • Supabase provides the database, sign-in, and file storage. Accounts, messages, voice messages, photos, profile photos, and the setup for a call are kept there. Supabase also delivers new messages and call setup to the people in the chat.
  • Google handles Continue with Google. The app also loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which receives the network address of the device that opens the page. If the voice relay is turned off, a call can use Google's public address lookup (STUN). That lookup sees the network address needed to connect the call. It does not receive your messages.
  • Metered provides the TURN relay for voice calls when the two devices cannot connect directly, which is common on mobile networks. The relay carries the call audio while the call lasts. It does not receive your messages or your profile.

How long data is kept

Information is kept while your account is active. To delete your account and the data stored with it, email ahsantaha16@gmail.com. We will delete the account and that data.

Security

Chats are limited to the people in them. Sign-in and storage use encrypted connections. Choose a password that is hard to guess, and keep your device locked. No online service can promise perfect security.

Children

Athera Talks is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated, and the date at the top will change.
